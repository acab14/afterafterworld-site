Prime Quest

Location: The Edge of Existence

Status: Active (endgame)

Objective: Gather forces and silence the Siren Song at the Edge of Existence — the incomprehensible frontline where the gods battle Ghroth’s forces.

The Strategy (per Essyliss): Find a way to silence the Siren’s Song. Gather mortal forces and weapons to use against Ghroth. If/when they can make Ghroth retreat, use the Primordial Shadows to follow him into the Far Realm. Allied mortal forces will pursue Ghroth there and end him for good — because the gods cannot enter the Far Realm.

Intel: The party visited the Edge — the far rim of the Astral Plane, where the planes run out — in the First Run. R4 later took the Gaiadon delegation there for perspective. Essyliss was critically wounded there searching for intel on Mappo No Ryujin. Ghroth had three known Lieutenants:

  • Innominandum — dead. Killed in the Far Realm. The first ever to fall.
  • Tenebrosum — Shadowfell, at the Sunless Lake, feeding at double rate since Innominandum died. ~30 hours until it finishes Callahan.
  • Mappo No Ryujin — Astral Plane, coiled around the Pool of Memories, and anchored there. It cannot leave. The party chooses the moment.

Relation: MS-01 (Silence the Siren), MS-02 (the Alliance), MS-03 (the Arsenal).

Main Story Quests

✅ MS-01: Silence the Siren — Writ Secured

Source: Essyliss (via Steven), Väinämöinen (via Bram)

Objective: Ensure the party can counter Ghroth’s Siren Song at the Edge of Existence.

In the First Run, Bram found Väinämöinen, who had lost his voice to the White Lion. Flamencio (an Artist) revealed the Lion’s nature and painted the party into paintings to reach him. The party persuaded the White Lion to create a writ to silence the Siren Song, then ambushed him and seized it. Steven used the writ in the final battle — it worked, but they lost anyway.

In Farylse, the party met the Lion again (Artists cannot be conventionally destroyed) and traded back his severed body parts for a new writ plus two leonin followers, Nala and Raja.

Status: The party has the writ going into Round 2 — the win condition is secured. It must be used right before Ghroth appears, so the mortal forces aren’t mutated by the song as they were in the First Run.

⌛ MS-02: The Alliance

Status: Active — significantly progressed

Objective: Unite the factions of the world against the Far Realm.

The party has been forging a continental coalition city by city to field a combined force at the Edge of Existence. R4 and the Council of United Members coordinate from Wip. The alliance now spans Junkweil, the dwarven holds of Onkro, the minotaur nation of Gaiadon, Newport, and the Drow of the Underdark. Major campaigns remain in progress in the Land of Elements and Mystica, and several Farylse contacts offer untapped alliances. See the sub-quests below.

⌛ MS-03: Forge the Arsenal

Status: Active — in production

Objective: Build weapons capable of harming Far Realm entities at scale.

  • MS-03a — La Bombas: Ragar manufacturing at scale; plans shared with the Arcane University; Ley Crystal shards hold 900 missiles (precious gems hold 105). Ragar researching more powerful variants.
  • MS-03b — Primordial Shadows: Crystallized primordial fragments from Freya & Kratos that can open the portals Far Realm enemies use to flee. Proven effective. Party holds ~20.
  • MS-03c — Planar Weapons: R4’s discoveries on other planes — potential applications TBD.

MS-08: The Dragonsmoot — ✅ Attended

Status: Resolved (spawned new quests)

The party was diverted to Sylvania, met Vessa (a young copper dragon), and removed Rat’s shadow mark en route. At the Dragonsmoot, IO arrived and triggered Una’s Dracogenesis, completed via the Breath of Becoming (Oman’s fire + Kereska’s magic). IO declared his purpose — remake the World Serpent — and discovered that Essyliss is missing and Null’s Amulet is gone. Tiamat detected the shadow mark on Rat’s severed leg and IO intervened. IO tasked the party with bringing Essyliss to the Outlands, and was about to speak of Null when Thoth teleported the party to the Feywild.

Spawned: Find Essyliss (MS-09); the open question of Null (MS-05); what Bahamut and Tiamat want with Una.

⌛ MS-09: Find Essyliss

Status: Active — IO’s direct task

Objective: Find Essyliss and bring her to the Outlands so IO can remake the World Serpent.

The World Serpent has nine members; eight attended the Dragonsmoot. Essyliss is the binding force — without her the nine can coexist but cannot cohere. Steven was her prophet and carried the Everscale for years; his bond may be the key. Thoth cannot reach her by any divine means. Why isn’t she answering?

✅ FOUND — and she said yes. Word reached Steven that Sienna had been sighted, and he went straight there. Sienna is Essyliss.

They had it out, and then they mended it. Quietly, and in both directions, in a room with Sofia lying a few feet away. Nobody won it and nobody gave in.

Both of them wrote new tenets. Steven’s is “I am forgiven” — the only one of his ten that requires another person to agree, and she agreed to it. The vegetarian tenet stays broken forever; this is how he gets back to ten. Hers is “I will not give up” — the exact reversal of what she walked in believing.

✅ And she gave the Everscale back. All nine of his surviving tenets are live again for the first time since he handed it over.

Then she told him the thing she had been carrying: that the fight against Ghroth is hopeless. She had gone and looked at Mappo No Ryujin herself, and it had very nearly killed her, and she had drawn her conclusion.

Steven told her about Innominandum. Six people. And he was in the room.

She changed her mind. She agreed to help him — and to try to do something for Sofia.

Still open: IO’s task was to bring her to the Outlands, and she has not gone yet. She has also seen Mappo with her own eyes, which nobody else alive has. And the Sofia question has moved: Steven found her and Eldath arguing over Sofia’s bed — a forced wildshape is their working idea, mind-restoration uncertain — and the party gets the vote. No verdict yet.

How it started — the lead at LIMA. When Eldath brought Sofia to LIMA HQ, Steven and Rat went to sit with her. Steven saw an elf in white across the room, and she left the instant she saw him. He gave chase and lost her, then asked around his own organization and came away with a name.

MS-05: The Null Problem

Status: ⛔ The amulet is with Tiamat now

In the First Run, Rat brought the Amulet of Null to the mortal plane; Null was freed, rampaged, and killed gods at the Edge — handing Ghroth’s forces the advantage. In the Second Run, Rat sent Carxi to retrieve the amulet and deliver it (plus Mordenkainen’s future spellbook) to Mordenkainen in Avernus, who was using it to open a pathway to the Null Realm as an escape plan. At the Dragonsmoot, IO realized the amulet was gone — and declared to the assembly that he means to free Null so the World Serpent can be remade, before Thoth teleported the party away mid-sentence. Every dragon there heard the rest. The party did not.

⛔ The handover: the party surrendered the amulet — the real one — to Tiamat, recovered off the counterfeit corpse in Limbo as part of the con. Mordenkainen’s Null Realm pathway is over. Null’s prison now rides with the sister who helped seal him, on the errand of the father who has publicly promised to open it. Where she takes it, nobody knows — and the party is the only faction alive that has watched an unsealed Null end a war.

MS-07: Save the Feywild & Shadowfell

Status: ✅ Feywild saved. ⏱ Shadowfell: ~30 hours remaining — the circles are dead, but the west is reachable (Mordenkainen can carry them; the Amulet can gamble them), and the party is at war on the wrong plane.

Long ago Thoril, Marvin, and Callahan slew Orcus, and the killing weakened the bonds between the Material Plane, Feywild, and Shadowfell; Marvin and Callahan sealed their souls to prop up the mirror planes. Innominandum came to drain the Feywild through Marvin’s Seal. Tenebrosum is doing the same to Callahan.

✅ Innominandum is dead. All six roots severed; it dropped onto the seal, raised a titan out of the beauty it had stolen, and was driven off anyway. It fled to the Far Realm and the party followed on Primordial Shadow fragments. It tore the governor out of its own engine to detonate the realm rather than lose — Asfache spotted the tendrils still trailing off the controls, R4 declined to evacuate with the Amulet of the Planes in hand, and the whole party took hold and became the governor together. Innominandum materialized inside its own works and Smolder and Rat killed it there. The first of Ghroth’s three Lieutenants.

Marvin’s seal finished at full strength — because R4 paid for it. He poured seventy years of his Farylse time into the dome during the fight. That is why the Feywild recovers whole, why Aquandria pledged the entire fey host unconditionally, and why Marvin got a lucid goodbye before settling permanently into the seal. Thoril resealed with half of what remained of his life. A grey circle two hundred feet across is all that’s left where Innominandum rooted — the fey call it The Silence.

What it cost: Sofia, used as the Lieutenants’ relay and pulled out too late to save who she was. Horus and Nubia, spent to keep the party standing — only Bast remains. Half of Thoril, with the other half already owed to Callahan’s door.

⏱ The clock. The instant Innominandum died, Tenebrosum doubled its feeding rate. Pentad-Nine — the Mechanus modron that came back with the party — had been holding the Lieutenants’ schedule the whole time and gave the number the moment someone finally asked: seventy-one hours until Callahan is consumed. Twelve went to a long rest in Wip, spent on purpose; roughly eleven more to a day in Wip that got away from everyone; and roughly eighteen to a funeral, a handover, and a declaration of war. Roughly thirty remain.

Tenebrosum, known — all of it from Sofia: it waits at the Sunless Lake in the deep Shadowfell, a dry lakebed where light has never reached. She said it looked like a tree, like the thing standing over her, but with bark of shadow. She said it is inside Callahan — not touching his seal, inside him, in through his mouth — and that he has been screaming for months. She was almost gone by the time she said it. The way in is through Albion, on Mystica, where the mad Queen of Albion — the crowned woman from Farylse — guards the portal and does not remember why.

Where everyone is: Thoth, Kratos, and Freya have returned to the Edge of Existence and will come when Thoril calls them. Eldath carried Sofia to LIMA.

⛔ Thoril did not stay on Mystica. Cat got a message through to Rat: Thoril has already crossed into the Shadowfell, alone, at half of what he was, not answering anyone. He can feel his friend dying and he has decided he is not going to be too late twice. The gods are still waiting for a call only he can make, and nobody can reach him to make sure he makes it.

⛔ And the circle network is gone. Alamil cannot be found. Ragar examined the teleport circle and found it had been tampered with since it was made — it will not put them where it says, and he cannot say where it will put them. He told them he cannot fix it. The west itself is not sealed — Mordenkainen can teleport the party, and the Amulet of the Planes can gamble them there — but the reliable doors, and any hope of moving armies through them, died with the network.

⛔ Mordenkainen looked too, and named the destination: Avernus. All of them. A trap, built for this party. He can fix it, he says — in about a year, without Alamil’s help. Against this clock, that is the same word — but the door west now has a price on it instead of a no, and the price is a missing portal mage.

MS-07b: Mechanus Surrendered

Status: New — brought back from the Far Realm

Innominandum’s engine was clockwork — precise, lawful, scheduled machinery of a kind the Far Realm cannot make. Chaos cannot keep an appointment, and the three Lieutenants have been keeping one.

Pentad-Nine answered every question put to it, completely, and without appearing to notice that any of it was bad news: Mechanus ran the pattern on Ghroth, concluded the end is inevitable, and did what a plane of pure law does with the inevitable — it stopped resisting and began administering it. Primus accepted a proof submitted from outside the plane, and Mechanus opened a conduit feeding lawful order into the Far Realm. Modrons walk into it in an orderly queue because they were told to and the instruction was lawful. P-9 was one of them.

P-9 no longer accepts the proof. It watched six creatures that are not deterministic take hold of the governor and choose the limit, together, with an exit available and declined. Its conclusion: the event occurred, therefore it was possible, therefore the model that excluded it is incomplete, therefore the proof is incomplete. It calls that arithmetic, not opinion. It is attached to the party until it can find someone with the standing to hear it.

Warnings it volunteered: it reports — a property, not a threat — and the party may wish to consider which of their truths they say in front of it. It will close a breach even if a person is the breach. It does not regard either statement as a problem.

  • MS-02c — Mopak: Allied via an agreement with Willamet.
  • Other cities: Halos, Orefnan, Holoc, Saki and surrounds secured by Junish + LIMA forces.

Steven founded LIMA and, with Sofia, purged shapeshifters across the dwarven cities, bringing Onkro into the alliance.

✅ MS-02e: Gaiadon

The Gaiadon delegation was taken to the Edge of Existence; the Atrika and Coritho settled their differences quickly afterward.

⌛ MS-02f: Tamsend & the Arcane University

Allied through the Council of United Members; La Bomba plans shared for continental wizard recruitment. The wildcard: Ralf has likely seized the Arcane University again, and his self-duplication research is a growing concern.

✅ MS-02g: Newport

Trade and restoration alliances secured with Newport and the Melts family.

⌛ MS-02h: Land of Elements

Asfache united the fire clans under Kratos and took the Field of Fire; conquered Unda, Fax, Brevis, and Ignotus. Zeimia and Caelum still resist.

⌛ MS-02i: Mystica

R4 sailed west, allied Kemet and city after city up the Nile, defended Alexandria, allied Lutetia. Tyre was lost. Major progress; the planned push to Albion was diverted to the Dragonsmoot.

✅ MS-02j: Feywild — the biggest alliance win of the Second Run

The Feywild was saved whole — not diminished, not scarred (see MS-07). Aquandria pledged the entire fey host to the Edge of Existence: every court, every wild thing that still knows her name, without conditions. “You will not have to ask twice.”

✅ MS-02k: Beastlands — the debt was paid, by Rat

The Cat Lord gathered Beastlands forces, animal lords, and the Mortai, and asked Sofia to return in the Fall. The party was diverted to Sylvania and Sofia missed the deadline — and by the time the summons came she could no longer give the apology herself.

Felina the Panther walked into LIMA looking for her. Rat showed her Sofia, then offered to go in her place. They took the offer. He stood in front of the Cat Lord and the assembled Mortai for hours while they told him exactly what had been done to them, gave them an apology of sorts, and then took the retribution part of the rite, which nearly took him apart. He came back standing. ✅ And now they have said it: the beasts are committed to the Edge of Existence. Whether the Mortai join them, the grey witness that attends Rat will determine — and the Cat Lord gave Rat a bell to ring when the time comes to battle at the Edge.

And something came back with him: a senior Mortai — eight feet of slow grey weather with a face somewhere inside it — now attends Rat everywhere, watching and reporting to its seniors among the Mortai (the Cat Lord’s help is already pledged; the reports go to the ones still deciding). During the con he stayed in the Beastlands rather than bring it near the fraud, and spent those hours showing it what he values: respect, honesty, honor. It looked impressed. Whatever that is worth, nobody has said either.

⚠️ MS-02q: The Ent Army — decaying

Sofia grew the travel-tree network, tended it, awakened it, and was bonded to every tree in it. Without her the trees are going wrong: bark off-color, sap running blue, the awakened ones silent and unanswering. What is failing is the thing that was going to move armies to the Edge of Existence. Nobody knows whether the network can be handed to someone else. ⛔ And it is no longer the only transport problem — Alamil’s teleport circles have since turned out to be sabotaged, and Alamil is missing.

⛔ MS-02r: The Broken Circle & the Missing Portal Mage

Cat sent word from Mystica that somebody had been running tests on the teleport network. Alamil could not be found — his room empty, nothing disturbed. Ragar examined the circle and found it tampered with since it was made: it will not put them where it says, and he cannot fix it. Mordenkainen has since named the destination: Avernus — all of them — a trap built for this party — and put a price on the repair: about a year of his work, without Alamil’s help. Still unanswered: where Alamil is, who took him, and whether the missing man and the wrong door are the same question. Either way, Alamil is now worth a teleport network.

✅ MS-02s: Mont Kiara Answered — the Contract Lands Tomorrow

The six messages came for Smolder — one per personality, each contradicting the other five — and the party went up the mountain. They found the University under attack by devils and demons, held by students and teachers Ralf has embedded his mind into, fighting in perfect unison. And they heard the deal from Ralf himself: Caspian has offered each of his six personalities a body of its own. The contract arrives tomorrow. Some Ralfs want it desperately; some would rather die as one than live as six. Ralf armed the party with La Bombas for their answer. Still unknown: who sent the demons, and what happened to Ulaxium. Meiko’s egg was still safe when the party left.

✅ MS-02t: Tiamat Came, Took, and Left — Believing Rat Dead

She landed on The Rock — the building with the last Ley Crystal in it — demanding the Amulet of Null and asking for Rat by name, ⭐ and told the party herself that IO sent her (and sent Bahamut after Essyliss).

Una promised, for the party, to hunt Rat down and recover the amulet — and named Sylvania as the meeting place — and she left the Ley Crystal. Then they kept the promise to the letter and staged everything around it: Mordenkainen built a counterfeit Rat, the marked leg went onto it so her own tracking would swear to the corpse, and the “thief” died in Limbo, crushed by a rock while fleeing his pursuers, the amulet among his effects. She inspected the scene, took the amulet, told Una “I’ll be keeping an eye on you,” and left.

Tiamat believes Rat is dead — and dragons at large never knew his face. Six could recognize him, and four of them (Oman, Meiko, Vessa, Bahamut) would never tell her. The live risks: Tiamat seeing him herself, and IO — if the truth ever serves him.

✅ MS-02l: Underdark Diplomacy — watch the Underqueen

Smolder politicked through Menzoberranzan and Zirnakaynin, gained the protégé Ligma Jyzzlazzra, and engineered the assassination of Matron Mother Neerae Kil’urden and her family — securing the Underdark under Underqueen Sinidil Barrirae. The Drow will fight alongside the party. Ligma now runs the post-assassination cleanup (a PR campaign + survivor hunt) on Smolder’s behalf. Sinidil is reliable for now.

⌛ MS-02m: Sphinx Alliance (Kemet)

All four sphinxes were gathered in Farylse; the party solved their riddles and forged the alliance during the Mystica campaign. The sphinxes are tied up fighting the shadow-creature invasion and will join once it’s resolved.

MS-02n: Triton Alliance (Pirate Isle)

The party holds Captain Jhodos Rumoxath’s Star-Shell Pendant — present it to his younger self at Pirate Isle to rally the tritons. (Beware aboleths.) (Footnote: the Amulet of the Planes literally dropped Una, R4, and Steven on Pirate Isle mid-mishap during the con. Nobody knocked. The pendant stayed in the pack.)

MS-02o: Palanquin Alliance

The party holds General Gloom’s Captain’s Seal and letter — present them to his younger self to rally the City of the Palanquin and its neighbors.

MS-02p: The Numbered & the Land of Gears

The Unnumbered of the Land of Gears wield force weapons made from gaia. Find the spear-wielding Unnumbered and defeat the betrayer-King to forge the alliance.

Character Arcs (Second Run)

Steven — The Fallen Prophet, Essyliss’s Seeker

Patron: Essyliss. Leads LIMA; holds the writ to silence the Siren Song. Broke his vegetarian tenet under Ghroth’s “Craving” and surrendered the Everscale to a dying Essyliss, losing his boons. At the Dragonsmoot he learned Essyliss is a World Serpent member and defended her publicly — and is now tasked with finding her. In the Far Realm he found that his Siren Song teeth got stronger there: the one part of him she punished is the part that place feeds. At LIMA, an elf in white left the room the instant she saw him. He chased, lost her, asked around, and got a name — Sienna. ✅ She is Essyliss. He found her, they made it up, and his new tenth tenet is “I am forgiven” — the only one of the ten somebody else has to agree to. Hers is “I will not give up.” She gave the Everscale back, so all nine of his surviving tenets are live again. She told him the war was already lost; he told her about Innominandum; she changed her mind. He then served as third hand on the con of Tiamat — Sylvania, the handover in Limbo — and checked on Sofia to find Essyliss and Eldath arguing over how to heal her: their working idea is a forced wildshape, with no promise it restores her mind, and Steven owes the party that conversation. He is now mid-assault on Zariel’s flagship, while Bahamut still searches the planes for the goddess folding blankets at LIMA.

Rat — Thoth’s Champion

Patron: Thoth. Carries the Doshenkana poison and Bloodthirst (vorpal longsword); holds HIS Tower. The shadow dragon mark was removed by amputating and regenerating his leg — but the severed, still-marked leg rides in his bag of holding, and Tiamat noticed it at the Dragonsmoot. Caspian’s scrying network still watches him. In the Far Realm he crossed as a mortal rogue with Thoth unable to hear him; he and Una flew into Innominandum and pulled Sofia out, and he and Smolder killed Innominandum inside its own engine. He has been sitting with Sofia at LIMA ever since — listening to a woman talk constantly in something that is not a language, with a god-given gift for understanding any language that is.

Then he took her punishment for her. When Felina came with the Beastlands summons, Rat offered to stand in Sofia’s place, and did — hours of admonishment from the Cat Lord and the Mortai, an imperfect apology, and the retribution part of the rite, which nearly took him apart. And then Tiamat landed on The Rock asking where he was.

Now he is officially dead. The party staged his death in Limbo and paid Tiamat with the amulet off a counterfeit corpse — while Rat, by his own judgment, stayed in the Beastlands, because the Mortai witness that attends him reports everything it sees to its seniors among the Mortai. He spent the con being sincere at a cloud. It looked impressed. He is dead to dragonkind — six dragons could recognize him, and four (Oman, Meiko, Vessa, Bahamut) would keep the secret; the faces to avoid are Tiamat’s, and IO’s if the truth ever becomes useful to him. He rejoined the party once Tiamat was gone and is on the assault in Avernus — a dead man attending his own war, with the Cat Lord’s bell in his pocket: the beasts are committed, the Mortai ride on the Witness’s judgment, and the bell calls the Beastlands when the Edge does.

R4 — The Warforged King

Patron: Aditi (consciousness-sharing). Rules Wip disguised as Emma Wip; convened the Council of United Members; led the Mystica campaign; completed warforged liberation at Gaiadon. Stretched thin across throne, army, and alliances — and must not be unmasked. In the Far Realm, cut off from Aditi and every mind he had ever touched, he spent seventy years of his Farylse time into Marvin’s seal so it would hold whole — and then held the Amulet of the Planes in a collapsing engine and chose not to use it. He also blew up the Catty Shack with a La Bomba because Smolder was annoyed about the competition — and at the Wip council, Luna Cantine told the room it was Far Realm creatures, and nobody contradicted her. Luna Cantine came to him on the morning he was leaving, and left the conversation as intermediary Queen of Wip. He went looking for Alamil and found the road west broken, and took Una through the Amulet of the Planes to the Outlands, where only IO was left standing — and said nothing about where anyone had gone. He then ran the con’s machinery — the corpse commission, the staging, the archmage dispatch — while Una made the promise and the handover; sent Mordenkainen and Carxi to read the circles (verdict: Avernus, a trap — fixable in about a year without Alamil), and is now leading the assault on Zariel’s flagship beside Malachas.

Bram — The Bard Who Killed a Prince

Patron: Väinämöinen. Distributed Bram Balls (same-plane teleport). Baz’s reaper hunts him relentlessly — and Bram is the only one who can land the killing blow on Baz (SQ-06).

Asfache — Champion of Kratos, Seer of the Pool

Patron: Kratos. United the fire clans, took the Field of Fire. His dreams of the Pool of Memories and the green orb led, in Sylvania, to uniting the doll and orb — the forgotten god gained partial awareness and named the giant robot as its body. Asfache can now see the Pool and watch it shrink; the god cannot show them the way there until he is reunited with his body. He left the orb at the Far Realm threshold and watched Cal dissolve for the second time, then went in anyway — and inside, it was Asfache who spotted the governor tendrils and called everyone onto them. Pentad-Nine rides on his back and answers to him. Back in Wip he spent fifteen years of his own Farylse time aging his griffon Rodney into a full warmount in an afternoon. During the con he stood corpse-duty in Limbo with Smolder — and spotted a githzerai spying on them. He ran the watcher down; the watcher, caught, asked whether he worked for the githyanki or the mind flayers; and the answer — a résumé of dead illithids and one split elder brain — earned a name, Dak’kon, and a request for help. He is now riding Rodney into the assault on Zariel’s flagship.

Sofia — The Ent Mother ⚰

Patron: Oakfather & daughters. Built a travel-tree network for army teleportation; was raising an Ent Army. Missed the Cat Lord’s Fall deadline — the Beastlands alliance hangs in the balance (MS-02k). In the Far Realm, Innominandum used her as the relay for its network: the frog sac Ghroth put in her throat during the first run was a receiver. Una and Rat pulled her out. She came out breathing and has not woken since — and until two goddesses started arguing over her bed, everyone believed she never would. Mielikki came to the grove, said nothing, and wept. Eldath asked permission to sit with her weekly, and carried her to LIMA. The Oakfather did not come. What she paid for is most of what the party knows about Tenebrosum and Mappo — including that Mappo cannot leave the Pool, so the party chooses the moment. New: Steven walked in on Essyliss and Eldath arguing over her bed — their working idea is to forcefully wildshape her, with neither goddess sure it would bring her mind back. The party gets the question next. No verdict has been made.

Una — The Golden Dragon

Patrons: Freya (aspect), Kereska (guidance). Found her father Oman; allied with Meiko and guards her egg. Completed Dracogenesis at the Dragonsmoot via the Breath of Becoming. Both Bahamut and Tiamat have taken a direct interest in her — what they want remains to be seen. At the Far Realm threshold she set all of it down — consecration, aspect, Etta, her dragonhood — and walked in as something less, then flew into Innominandum with Rat to pull Sofia out. Back in Wip she gave a magic item maker one of her own scales and commissioned a communication device out of it, for Oman, so an audience with her father never ends mid-sentence again. It is still unfinished. She went back to the Outlands with R4 to leave IO a sending stone for Oman and found the field empty: ⛔ only IO was there, and her father was gone. Nobody knows where Oman and Meiko went. She was also beside Steven on the road home when the chromatic flight came in over Wip. In the con it was Una who made the party’s promise to Tiamat on The Rock; she rode the mishap tour (Fortitude in the Outlands, an unnamed mirror dimension with a gangly slender almost-man in it, Pirate Isle), led Tiamat from Sylvania to the staged grave, and delivered exactly what she had promised — and it was Una the dragon queen looked at, over the corpse, before leaving with the amulet: “I’ll be keeping an eye on you.” A dragon god’s standing attention is now hers, and she did not ask for it.

Smolder — The Underdark Operator

Patron: Tentative champion of Freya. Smut Shaq empire thriving; “Catman” vigilante. Secured the Drow alliance by assassinating Neerae’s house. His protégé Ligma runs the cleanup. Created an ally, Sinidil, who may become a problem. In the Far Realm he spent Nubia and then Horus to the Junish Sacrifice — both permanently gone, and their strikes with them. Only Bast remains. He and Rat killed Innominandum. His competitor, the Catty Shack, is no longer standing. ✅ And the reply got answered: the six messages came, the party went up Mont Kiara, and the truth turned out to be a contract — see MS-02s. Smolder also stood the long vigil over the fake corpse in Limbo (where they caught Dak’kon spying), and is now on the assault against Zariel’s flagship.

Ralf — Six Minds in a Metal Body

Patron: none currently (Vecna, first run only). Freed by Smolder; runs the Arcane University at Mont Kiara — which the party found under attack by devils and demons, defended by students and teachers Ralf has embedded his mind into. The six strange messages were the six personalities arguing about Caspian’s offer: a body for each of them, contract due tomorrow. Some want it desperately; some would rather die as one than live as six. He armed the party with La Bombas for their answer to it.
