The Known World

The poster edition, and the best single picture of the world there is: Topoda in the northwest, Mystica across the middle sea, the home archipelago off to the northeast, and the southern continents — Caelum, Unda, Fax, Ignotus, Brevis — that Asfache spent five years walking. Inset panels for Gaiadon and the Pirate Isles, which sit far to the south. The little land marked FARYLE out in the Mortem Ocean is spelled the way the mapmaker heard it.

The world as the party knows it — commissioned by King Ramses from the mapmaker Tingle, derived from the map table in Jun. Everything in silhouette is still out there.

The same world to scale. The campaign began on the little cluster in the upper right.

The same map with the GM’s pen on it, for anyone who has lost track: T is Topoda, M is Mystica, LoE the Land of Elements, Yore and Eara are themselves, the blue circle is home, and the red circle is the Pirate Isles.

A high-resolution version of the world map lives here on Google Drive.

The Home Archipelago

Junkweil and its neighbors: Onkro, Tamsend, Gaiadon, Newport, and the volcano isle of Sanddown.

Junkweil, Hand-Drawn

The original table map of Junkweil — Lake Junkweil to the Forbidden Jungle, with the Riverway and the Two Kings Road. Pirate Isle is the one with the skull.

Wip, the Capital

Wip’s districts — Cleargate, The Rock, The Harbor, Westgate, Fishsong, Ghostforge, Moongate, Glassplace — and the sewer lines under them, which have mattered more than once.

The citadel under The Rock: from the War Room down through the Underwell to the Mines.

Mystica

The Second Run abroad: Albion, Gaul, Djermani, Istria, Kemet — and the road the party traveled from Albion down to Tyre.

The Kemet reach: Alexandria, Lycopolis, Crocodilopolis, Thebes, and the river between.

The same continent drawn by nation and by weather — Albion in red, Gaul in green, Istria in purple, Hellas in orange, Kemet in blue along its river. Hot in the west, cold in the east, and the Isle of the Tempest sitting offshore in weather of its own.

Topoda

All of Topoda, named: the Kitami Tundra and Warnhome Pass in the north, the Protected Lands in the middle, the Ethereal Wastes and Q’dar in the south, the Land of Gears across the water — and the Neverfog on the western edge, which is exactly as helpful as it sounds.

That coast up close: Treetop Town, Ana’s Wood, and Weresharks Grotto — where the whole story started, before the clock did.

The Land of Yore

The Land of Yore: Glarsht, Bram’s family home, on the Sovth River.

The Land of Elements

The Land of Elements as it was first charted, before the party finished exploring it: Viavolca Way, the Volcarun Hills — and the Edge of Existence.

The Pirate Isles

A small scatter of islands in the southern ocean, south of the Land of Elements and a very long way from home — harbours, camps, a mine, a volcano, and one skull. Nobody has written the names in. Jhodos Rumoxath’s younger self is somewhere on them, and the aboleths are somewhere in the water around them.

State of the World

The Year 1 status map, with the key: religion marks for the Oakfather, Freya, and Essyliss; stability dots — green thriving, orange stable, red unstable, black unknown.

Two later snapshots of the same tracking — note the green dot at Wip, and the red one at Jun. Jun knows what a broken crystal costs.
