Ralf

Ralf before: a dwarf, a hammer, and exactly one personality. Then Etheniss’s sabotage killed him in the Soul of the Rock, and what walked back out was an igloid body, a fistful of Ley Crystal shards — and the same hat.

Una

Una

“I let my hair cover my other eye, for drama.”

Steven

*Lizard, Laugh, Love. Nine of the ten tenets are spoken. This one he had inked.*
