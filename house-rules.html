La Bomba

The magic missile bomb. Three steps: build a receptacle, channel magic missiles into it, set it off.

1 · Creating the receptacle

Time: construction takes 25 days, reduced by the Arcana score of each character actively working. If someone stops working, their Arcana score is added back to the days remaining. Example: Ralf (6), Una (3), and Ragar (7) working together take 16 days off the total — 9 days of all-day construction and incantation.

Construction: each day, everyone working rolls Arcana and adds the results together. Collectively beat DC 30 and a day is subtracted from the total. Fail, and roll a mishap (1d100).

Receptacle: one pound of material, shaped into an orb and prepped with runes. The material sets the maximum missiles it can hold:

Material Max missiles
Copper 3
Silver 6
Iron 12
Steel 24
Electrum 27
Gold 54
Mithral 60
Platinum 72
Precious gems 105

2 · Channeling missiles into the receptacle

You must be able to cast magic missile to fill a receptacle. Adding a cast takes 2 hours per spell level — or spend 24 hours and hit a DC 18 Arcana check to add all of your spell slots for the day (the “All Day Add”; fail the check and you only add slots up to 4th level). Broken concentration on a single add: the missiles are not added and the slot is expended. Broken concentration during an All Day Add: you lose half your spell slots, your choice of levels.

Blast radius: 10-ft radius once the bomb holds 10 missiles, +10 ft per additional 10 missiles. Range: 120 ft, +10 ft per 10 missiles.

3 · Casting La Bomba

The caster must be holding the orb and must be able to cast a 1st-level spell — you do not need to know magic missile to set one off. The caster spends 1 action concentrating per 10 missiles (minimum 1 round) and may do nothing else — no movement, no bonus action, no reaction — or the spell fails. If concentration breaks, the cast fails but the bomb is not spent. When the count is done, the release must come immediately (within 6 seconds) or the working collapses. After casting, the receptacle is expended.

Example: a fully filled gold receptacle (54 missiles) takes 5 turns of pure concentration; on the 6th turn, Una casts La Bomba — 54d4+54 force in a 50-ft radius, at up to 170 ft.

There are no timers and no fuses. A La Bomba goes off because somebody stood still in the middle of everything, long enough to finish. For what the party is currently carrying, see the Armory section of The War Ledger.

Firearms

Feats

Gunslinger

  • Gain proficiency with firearms and tinker’s tools.
  • On a misfire, you can use your reaction to roll a d20; beat the weapon’s misfire score and it doesn’t misfire. Once per short or long rest.
  • Load a firearm as a free action, if you have a free hand.
  • When you Attack with a one-handed weapon, you can use a bonus action to attack with a loaded light firearm you’re holding.
  • Ignore Heavy Kickback.

Trickshots (requires Gunslinger) Grit Points equal to your proficiency modifier, restored on a long rest. Spend one when firing (declared before the roll) to make a Trick Shot:

  • Deadeye — advantage on your next attack with a gun.
  • Winging Shot — knocks the target prone.
  • Incapacitating Shot — incapacitate the target until the end of its next turn.
  • Forceful Shot — push the target 5 ft in the direction of your shot.
  • Piercing Shot — if an enemy is within 5 ft of your target in the direction of the shot, make a second attack roll against it; on a hit, deal the same damage.
  • Violent Shot — misfire chance becomes 10 for this shot; on a hit, add your level + proficiency bonus to damage.

Gun Master (requires Gunslinger)

  • Load as a free action, no free hand needed.
  • Ignore the misfire property entirely.
  • Guns crit on 19–20.
  • Curve the Bullet — targets gain no benefit from half or three-quarters cover.

Blunderbuss

2d12 + DEX bludgeoning/piercing · 7 lbs · range 15/45 · Two-Handed (one-handed at STR 18) · Loading (bonus action) · Misfire 3

Misfire: on a misfire, add 1 misfire point; the gun can’t be used until you spend an action clearing the chamber. At 5 misfire points it explodes for 3d12 fire damage. Out of combat, spend 1 minute and make a DC 10 + misfire-number check (Dexterity or Intelligence with tinker’s tools) to reduce the gun’s misfire number by 1, to a minimum of its original score.

Heavy Kickback: firing with Strength below 13 forces a DC 10 STR check or you’re knocked prone. Already prone? Then it hardly matters, does it.

The table also plays with Drama Cards — a third-party deck by Tucker Long, found at tuckerlong.github.io/drama-cards.
