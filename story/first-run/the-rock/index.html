The Rock

A fortress that had never been breached, and four separate people with a plan to own it.

Back in the sewers, everyone came to the table: Cat and the Ghostbloods, Dryzzlazz and the Spiders, Luna and Skoll Cantine for the Wolves, Priscilla, Willamet, Blade, Silrael, and the party.

Catroniel had won the third seat, and he used it immediately. His plan, laid out plainly: get inside The Rock, empty its armory, arm the Guild and the Tinkers with what came out plus the guns from the Heartforge, and then drive Newport out of Wip en masse. He offered guns to the Cantines and the Spiders for their support, and asked for safe passage through the sewers. He said he had further plans he would share once they were inside.

What he needed was somebody to relieve the minotaur smugglers of their cargo and walk into The Rock wearing their reputation.

Everyone in the room turned and looked at the party.

The note

Cat took Rat aside more than once, and not everything he said was for the table.

He wanted an igloid captured for study. He wanted the layout of The Rock — how the security worked, where the armory was, what the guard looked like from the inside. He wanted Rat to be ready for a coup. And he wanted Rat to carry a note to whoever was commanding Newport at the negotiations, offering to accept Newport’s supremacy over the region so long as Cat was the one ruling Wip, with trade terms and shared gun technology attached.

Rat took the instructions. He had known Cat since he was a child. There was no reason on earth to doubt him.

Into the crates

They met the minotaur contact Thum’gad and talked their way into the shipment by hiding inside the crates.

Bram spotted the ambush a moment before it landed, and Smolder noticed something else entirely: the Wip officer running the exchange was being scried on. Newport igloids came out of the treeline in force, and the fight went badly enough that the party detonated an entire crate of guns to buy themselves an exit. Rat, still inside a crate at the time, shoveled everything that hadn’t exploded into the bag of holding.

Then a running fight through the forest — gunfire, soldiers, potholes, and a stampede of thoroughly spooked deer — with three surviving Rock soldiers named Jared, Thomas and Steve running beside them.

The run for the secret entrance. Two of the three soldiers did not make it.

Pyre at the door

At the secret entrance stood a red-haired man in a crown — Pyre Melts, eldest of the Melts children — beside a robed wizard named Vanthal Reminar, with a platoon waiting in the trees.

Una talked them into walking away from the door. The moment they were far enough out, the ruse frayed, Ralf gave the signal, the soldiers tackled Pyre and Vanthal, and Una convinced the surrounding troops that the two tacklers were the real threat. Smolder took Pyre’s golden crown of rubies off his head on the way past.

Everyone ran. Jared was burned to death. Thomas was disintegrated. Steve and the party made the tunnel and sealed it behind them, and the door held.

Una noticed, going in, that her magic felt muffled — the closer to The Rock, the quieter it got.

Emma Wip

The tunnel ran to a translucent purple wall with a brass horn set into it. On the far side stood a tall woman in a silver crown of welded emeralds, flanked by ten hulking figures in runed stone armor.

Emma Wip knew Steve by name. He told her what had happened at the ash tree — an ambush, a rearguard, a crate of guns going up — and Emma took the news standing. Then she asked who these people were, why they had helped him, and how many guns they had actually recovered. They answered all three, and she let them in.

The war room was worse than the view from outside. The Rock held five thousand soldiers. Newport had five thousand men and more than fifteen thousand igloids, with five manufacturing facilities in the city turning out five hundred a day.

So the party spread out what they had. Connections in the Thieves Guild. The Cantines and their wolves. And a spell called La Bomba — half of it, taken off a dead rakshasa’s desk.

Emma gave them the laboratory in the Soul of the Rock, and the wizard Ragar Huntsfellow to work with. And she gave Cat a seat on her council as Master of War.

Nine hundred missiles

Ralf, Una and Smolder worked the lab with Ragar and took La Bomba apart. It runs in three steps: build a receptacle orb, channel magic missiles into it over hours and days, then let the whole accumulated weight of it out at once.

The receptacle’s material decides how much it can hold. Precious gems will take a hundred and five missiles. A fragment of the Soul of the Rock — the city’s own Ley Crystal, sitting a room away — will take nine hundred.

That number has been in the party’s head ever since.

Meanwhile Asfache, Rat and Steven paraglided off The Rock into the city to keep the Guild sweet, and found out on arrival that Dryzzlazz’s arachnomancer handlers had abandoned her in the arena, and that the Spider Queen had gone back to being exactly as insane as she was before they got hold of her.

And in Ragar’s workshop, the party met one of his inventions: a warforged artificer, freshly awake, designated R41F.

The drill

Newport answered with a machine.

A Spider Drill — colossal, mechanical, three enormous bits grinding into the flank of The Rock, a cannon slung under its belly and repeating scatter guns bolted over every surface. A scouting team went out and confirmed the worst of it, including the timeline. It would be through the purple barrier in roughly twelve hours, and nobody knew how long the barrier would hold once it was touched.

Twelve hours to finish La Bomba. Emma, out of options, sent her Spymaster down to the lab to help.

Etheniss

Etheniss Orlytlar was a drow, and she had spent the siege quietly waiting for a reason to be in that room.

She helped. She genuinely helped. And while she helped she walked the chamber placing books, one at a time, all around the Ley Crystal — every one of them rigged with a Glyph of Warding. When the receptacle was filled she offered a toast, and the wine carried drow poison.

Alissa was killed by her death lance. Smolder saw the books for what they were, gathered every one of them up, and threw them into the furnace — which was the only thing to do with them and also detonated the entire set at once. The blast wrecked Ragar’s machines, damaged the Soul of the Rock, and immolated Ralf.

They killed Etheniss for it. Her purpose came out afterward, and it had never had anything to do with Newport: she was weakening the crystal to weaken the Underwell — the ancient seal, powered by the Soul of the Rock, that keeps the drow beneath the world from reaching the top of it.

She got what she wanted. Both the purple barrier and the Underwell were left leaking.

The Soul of the Rock. Smolder did the only thing you can do with a stack of rigged books, and it killed Ralf.

The hat

Afterward, in the wreckage, Ralf’s wizard hat was found intact.

Ragar had an igloid head on his bench. Smolder put the hat on it and pushed Ley Crystal shards into the eye sockets.

Ralf came back.

The Underwell

Below, the drow massed against a seal that no longer sealed. A priestess called up through the gap and announced that she had ten thousand troops standing ready to help the humans with their war, and asked, sweetly, why the barrier was only weakened and not fully down. She had, she said, heard from Etheniss.

Emma gave the party the choice: the threat above, or the threat below.

They went down. Mason Wip escorted them, and Captain Othelstan held the chamber, and the drow came up in waves — warriors on giant spiders, a mage riding a carrion crawler, and finally a fire giant climbing out of the hole. The party fought to get the cover onto the well while the well kept producing reasons not to.

Mason Wip was killed. Othelstan died, and stayed dead. Rat got the cover closed.

Then the forge crews arrived, hauling a massive iron forge cover to weigh the seal down for good — and behind them came three drow the party recognized from the Thieves Guild, dressed as cryptkeepers, carrying a map of The Rock’s secret tunnels and a note from Etheniss in Undercommon.

Otto

Above, through all of it, Bram and Otto had been flying a carpet over the battle for the city, dodging gunfire and harpoons and doing what the Ash brothers do.

A harpoon caught Otto and pulled him off it.

Bram went looking. He found his brother being tortured by Pyre Melts, who wanted to know where his crown had gone, and who killed him for not knowing. Bram got out, and mourned alone, and came back to the war room the next day and sang the battle for the people who had won it. They named him the aerial savior of Wip.

He did not tell them what the flight had cost him for a while.

The whistle

In the aftermath the numbers came in, and for once they were good: of the five igloid manufacturing facilities in the city, four had been destroyed.

Emma had also captured a Newport figure named Donny, and intended to trade him back to the Melts family for their withdrawal from Wip. When the Rock Guard seized him, something small fell out of his pocket and rang on the stone.

It was a whistle to Gamble Island.

Ancient Gamble Island

At the meeting, Pyre Melts made his own offer: ancient combat, to the death, over the crown Smolder had stolen off his head. Otherwise the siege continued.

Then Freya took a pair of temporal scissors to the moment, and the party landed on Gamble Island a very long time before there was anything on it. A port. A shack. Nothing else.

Inside the shack, a small man was dealing blackjack. They played, they asked questions, and they won a favor and a prize: a painting of an ear. This was the party’s first meeting with Flamencio, though he would not think of it that way when they met him again.

Then the Chronologians arrived — the time cops, for the first time, and none of the party had the faintest idea what they were looking at. Flamencio sent everybody home before it could escalate, having first been bribed with the transfer of Asfache’s smoothie business.

Ancient Gamble Island. The prize was a painting of an ear. The men in the doorway were the time cops.

The war room

They came back to a strangely empty Rock. The attendants were gone. Carxi met them and explained that everyone was preparing a celebration in the grand hall, and that the council was waiting for them in the war room.

Cat’s ambush was waiting in the war room.

Smolder killed Priscilla in the chaos. Cat killed Emma Wip — and then changed shape.

It had never been Catroniel. Not in the Heartforge, not at the Guild table, not in any of the quiet conversations with Rat. Every instruction Rat had been given, every map he had drawn, had been drawn for a thing wearing his mentor’s face.

Rat was turned to stone, briefly. A stranger named Alamil appeared in the middle of it, for reasons nobody understood at the time. And then the party killed the creature, and stood in a room with a dead queen in it.

The face

R4 picked up Emma’s crown and put it on.

He took her face with it. The crown that runs the purple barrier, the crown the Wip family has worn for generations, went onto a warforged who had been awake for about a week — and Wip has been ruled by a dead woman ever since.

The rest of the room moved just as fast. Luna Cantine got her family onto the council of Wip, and got R4’s approval for a discreet arrangement: Gemma and Peter Wip would be sent by ship to negotiate an alliance, and that ship would be attacked by pirates. Steven got Aussssten onto the council too, with Luna’s blessing, and the lizardfolk under the Glassplace acquired a protector at the top of the city.

And Rat heard voices for the first time. Several of them, overlapping, each claiming him. Then one underneath the others, deeper than the rest:

“No — he is mine.”

The party started calling him Dark Rat, and did not laugh very much when they said it.

The first council

R4 convened his first council meeting as the ruler of Wip, wearing a face he had owned for under a day. There were two items.

Ragar went first, and it was bad news. The Soul of the Rock had been damaged in Etheniss’s sabotage and was now slowly draining. The crystal powers the purple barrier, the barrier answers to the crown, and the same crystal holds the Underwell shut. How long either would hold, Ragar could not yet say.

Then Alamil arrived with the second item: the Arcane University’s Board of Directors had detected a powerful magical force in Wip, and were connecting it to the disappearance of a weapon prototype they had been developing. They were coming.

And somewhere in the middle of all of it, R4 heard a heartbeat, and a voice underneath it:

“Your sisters, your brothers… free them from Heart’s grasp!”

Aditi, goddess of consciousness, talking to a warforged about the fifteen thousand of his siblings currently being marched around the city as somebody’s furniture.

Elsewhere in the fortress, Flamencio dropped in on Asfache to tell him the smoothie business was taking off.

→ Jun, and the Battle of the Ziggurat — a clockwork dragon, a crystal that had held since the gods planted it, and the start of the five-year clock.
