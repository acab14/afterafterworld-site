Truth, and the Lion Who Shakes the Spear

A god’s errand finished at the front line, a party trick that saved a continent, and a robbery inside a painting.

Thoth heard that they had Fate and Chaos both, and took them to the Edge of Existence.

There is no useful way to describe it. An enormous fight, of incomprehensible size, in every direction, forever, with the gods in it. The party stood in the middle of that and waited while two deities had a conversation.

Tyr would give up the Aspect of Order — if the mortals were worth it. So the party made their case. Una argued, and argued well. Asfache demonstrated. Smolder reasoned out the tactics of the thing in front of them, out loud, to the god of justice, and got listened to.

Tyr handed the aspect over.

Thoth performed the ritual on the spot: Fate and Chaos, bound through Order. What came out the other side was Truth.

And the effect was immediate and absurdly simple. Every shapeshifter in the world now wore a thin purple-and-black aura, visible to anyone carrying the aspect’s power. Thoth granted it to all of them — and, better, made it transmissible, by the straightforward method of booping somebody on the forehead.

The Boop of Truth. A year of paranoia, solved by poking people.

Tap a forehead, see a shapeshifter. A continent’s worth of paranoia, solved by poking people.

Bram remembers

They booped Bram, and his memories came back with everything else.

He and his father had gone after the White Lion in Yore. They had been in the bushes, ready to strike. And a voice had sung:

“You will show where you’re hidden, Do as you’re bidden. Sing your songs no more, No sound, as before…”

Their hiding place stopped existing. They could not speak. And then:

“Back you go to friends and trade, Your memory here will wane and fade…”

Thoth listened to that and gave the party the only warning he had. The Lion Man is not a god. He is something outside of space and time, and Thoth did not know more than that. Be careful if you deal with him.

Then he sent them back to Thoril’s tavern, and thanked them for finishing a quest the gods themselves had not managed.

Thoril gets booped

They came back to find Thoril drunk and miserable again, so Smolder booped him with Truth.

He straightened up like a man remembering an appointment.

He was going to save his friends Marvin and Callahan, he said. Their essences are what keep the Feywild and the Shadowfell from collapsing. He thought he could help them.

He gave Smolder a set of teleporting handwraps that had belonged to Marvin, wished them luck, and left in a burst of light.

Flamencio

Then they went through his safes, because they are who they are.

They were heavily warded, and while the party was working on them, a small man appeared in the middle of the tavern and announced that he would LOVE to paint them all. They had seen him once before, on Ancient Gamble Island.

Flamencio helped with the wards on two of the safes in exchange for sitting. The safes gave up a very good shield and a pair of silk slippers; Sofia found a small chest in Thoril’s hop garden and planted a travel tree there while she was at it. And Flamencio painted each of them a 4x6 portrait, and while he worked, Bram asked him about the White Lion.

Flamencio’s answer reframed the entire war for about ninety seconds. The White Lion is like him. Neither of them is a god, or a Far Realm thing, or anything with a name in anyone’s cosmology. They draw their power from the artistry of everything in existence, and there are quite a few of them, and collectively they call themselves The Artists. Flamencio paints things into being. The White Lion writes things into being.

And Flamencio would happily take them to him. All they had to do was speak to the little paintings he’d just given them, and he would come.

Wip, briefly

They went home through Sofia’s trees and spread the Boop of Truth through the city of Wip, and by the end of it roughly half of R4’s advisors were dead, because roughly half of R4’s advisors had been shapeshifters.

They also picked up hearrings out of Newport — communication rings that work anywhere on the same plane, and have been standard party equipment ever since.

Somewhere in the middle of all this, Bram turned into Bram Balls. He has never satisfactorily explained it.

Bram Balls. Same-plane teleport charms, distributed liberally. Do not ask.

Then they sat down with R4 and made a plan to go and get a writ out of a being that exists outside creation.

The robbery

They spoke to their paintings, and Flamencio pulled them inside.

They came out on a hill in a strange land with the White Lion waiting just beyond it. Bram went in disguised. And between Bram and Una, they talked him into it — the Lion agreed to write a writ that would silence the Siren Song, on one condition. The party had to perform in his new play.

The Lion Who Shakes the Spear, keeping his word to people who had no intention of keeping theirs.

Then an earthquake hit. The Lion dropped the writ and fell into a crevasse.

The party took the writ and ran.

They also understood perfectly well that you cannot hide from a being who exists outside space and time, and that he was going to come looking. So they did not wait for him. They planned an ambush, threw everything they had into getting the drop on him, and got it.

They hacked off his limbs. Then his head.

They left the torso where it lay and teleported back to Wip with the one object in existence that could shut Ghroth up.

It is worth being honest about what that was. It was not a heroic act. It was a mugging, committed against something that had kept its word, by people who had run out of time to be nice.

They were not wrong about needing it.
