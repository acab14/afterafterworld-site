The Muster

A year and a half in which almost everything went right.

From the fall of Year 2 to the summer of Year 3, the party stopped adventuring and started building, and it worked.

The Boop of Truth spread outward from Wip city by city, taking Thoth’s following with it, and everywhere it went the shapeshifters stopped being invisible. Smolder’s Slums became an independent district under a deal with Liam, with a new Smut Shaq run by Bram’s sister Amelia. Rat and Carxi finally mended what killing her had broken — she gave him the Doshenkana poison and told him the tinkers had founded a town called Hemlock on the swamp’s edge to harvest poison components. Sofia awakened trees across the Land of Elements. R4’s goddess spoke to him and said she was proud of how many of their people he had freed, and that she would come to him in time.

And Una went home. She confronted her mother about the infidelity, made her tell her husband the truth, and watched it go badly — which is what happens, and is also where healing starts. Her mother is now infatuated with Steven. Her father regards Steven as his nemesis. Nobody has resolved this.

The Arcane University

Then they geared up and flew to Mont Kiara to find out what had eaten a school.

It was Ralf.

The place was a maze of traps and taunts and — eventually, once everyone had stopped setting things off — a negotiation. What came out of it was a three-way agreement between Ralf, the party, and Meiko, the ancient gold dragon he had been holding: Ralf lets Meiko leave, and in exchange he gets access to all of her gems. Una closed it by returning Meiko’s golden dragon egg. The party got Skol Cantine freed as part of the same deal, which is the answer to a question Luna had been asking for a year.

Una returned the egg. A three-way deal nobody trusted held together anyway.

Una also finally got the books, and learned the rest of it: she is undergoing Dracogenesis. She will become a golden dragon. If she wants it, she should be able to change back. Meiko made her promise to look after the egg if Meiko fell at the Edge of Existence.

And Ralf — who wanted the gems because he wanted to build La Bombas, as many as physically possible — asked the party for one thing in return. Go to the Starbold Hills, an extremely dangerous place, where there are creatures walking around with Ley Crystals implanted in them, and bring back as many crystals as they could carry.

They left with a paper-thin alliance and a shopping list, and winter arrived.

Malachas

The Arch Fiend’s contact stone appeared in Rat’s workshop precisely two weeks and one minute after the deal in HIS Tower, exactly as advertised, and the news it brought was a whole new map.

Two weeks and one minute, to the second. Arch Devils are punctual.

The Blood War is over. The Demon Lord Amadread went missing. Without him the Abyss collapsed and the demons were enslaved. And on the other side of the ledger, Zariel — ruler of Avernus — vanished along with her senior archfiends, because they had all gone to fight at the Edge of Existence.

That left a hole at the top of the Nine Hells, and something climbed into it. A rakshasa named Caspian.

The same Caspian who had dealt magic items to the party in Wip. The same Caspian Asfache had killed at Mont Kiara — because a rakshasa killed on the mortal plane does not stay dead; it reforms in the Hells. He had gone home, and then he had taken the place over.

Malachas wanted him gone, and wanted the party’s help doing it. That thread stayed live for the rest of the run.

The interrogation of the devils who had stolen Rat’s poison book had already pointed the same way: Caspian is keeping tabs on you.

Winter and the third year

Una and Steven spent the whole winter brokering a real peace with the Fey and training with them, and came away knowing Misty Step. Freya contacted Smolder: the dragons of the Land of Elements have an old arrangement with the primordials and can grant their power, but he had to prove himself on the Field of Fire first. He did. Rat and Bram invented a holiday — the Winter Day of Reflection, on which people gather, boop each other, and give “Pennies for your Thoths” — and Thoth’s power grew accordingly. Alamil put a teleport circle on an airship, which is the kind of idea that seems obvious only afterwards.

And Nyarlathotep came back and took the dwarven mountains again, so R4 started gathering an army.

In the spring of Year 3 they used it. The party trained the muster, went in, and beat Nyarlathotep in both realms — the mortal one and the Far Realm it had twice escaped into. The Essylissian volunteers organized into LIMA and stayed behind to help rebuild the dwarven cities, with Steven, Bram and Asfache working the surrounding boar cities into the alliance. Una and Smolder ran the Field of Fire together and came out stronger for it. Rat got the poison farm at Hemlock running properly.

Everybody missed Felafel that year. It is mentioned in the record with what can only be described as regret.

Summer, Year 3

The last full season before it all went wrong was the busiest of the run.

Asfache, Una and Steven went into the Feywild and found it full of tarrasques and monstrosities, and helped Thoril rescue Marvin. Thoril told them what he had learned: the Feywild and the Shadowfell are going to close in the spring of Year 4.

Rat took Ralf and Malachas into the Nine Hells and met Mordenkainen, who wanted a necklace of Tiamat’s in exchange for a legion of undead. They searched the Hells and they found it.

R4 and Smolder cured Dryzzlazz at last, and then used her: they built a story that Freya can lift Llolth’s curses, and sent it into the Underdark to see what would bite. R4 also assembled a consortium of the world’s rulers into a single unified front, trained the combined force, and pushed Nephren-Ka — who had taken Hemlock and the swamp back — all the way to her tower. He got every nation on board except the Land of Elements, and sent people to learn how to ask them properly.

Bram sang and storytold his way across Yore and convinced a third of the continent to fight.

Smolder went into the Underdark himself. The well was empty. He dropped in, met the emissary and an escort, and then the tunnels flooded and the aboleths came. He got badly hurt and lost three quarters of the drow force he was traveling with. He reached the priestess of the city-state anyway.

Meanwhile Alamil cut a portal to Yore for Bram, Ragar kept making La Bombas, and Luna and Skol organized the training of the largest army the world had assembled in living memory.

The thieves came back from the west with a report nobody wanted. The land out there is overrun with shadow monsters.

They were a year and two seasons from the deadline, with the writ in hand, the shapeshifters purged, the fey committed, an arsenal in production, and a continent under one banner.

It was, by every measure they had, going well.
