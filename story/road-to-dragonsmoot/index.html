The Road to the Dragonsmoot

The Mystica campaign, a detour to Sylvania, and a god who forgot its own name.

The Mystica campaign — Year 2 Redux

The second year pushed the war west, across the ocean to the continent of Mystica, where shadow creatures were swallowing the land. R4 led the expedition.

His fleet destroyed red dragons with La Bombas on the crossing and landed in Kemet, where they met the sphinxes they’d first encountered in Farylse — and answered the riddles that bought their alliance. City by city they fought up the Nile to Lycopolis, defended Alexandria through nights of shadow-creature assault (the things burn, and the party had firebenders), and allied with Alexander. Tyre was lost — and Ptolemy with it — but Lutetia came over after Steven charmed its ruler, Chlodio. The plan was to press on to Albion.

Two things interrupted that plan.

First, far away, Steven surrendered the Everscale — the scale Essyliss gave him, which carries his boons — back to the dying goddess he had served as prophet, and lost every one of them.

Second, the Amulet of the Planes failed.

Stranded in Sylvania

Bound for the Dragonsmoot, the party’s planar jump went wrong and dumped them in Sylvania instead. There they met Vessa, a young copper dragon, who immediately sensed two things on them: the Dracogenesis stirring in Una, and the shadow dragon mark burned into Rat. Vessa agreed to guide them onward.

The detour turned out to matter. Two long-running threads came to a head in Sylvania:

Rat’s mark. The chromatic residue of Tiamat’s shadow dragon had clung to Rat since the First Run, making him legible — and dangerous — to every dragon alive. So the party took the direct approach: they amputated Rat’s leg and regenerated it, cleanly removing the mark from his body. The severed, still-marked leg went into a bag of holding. A solved problem, and a new liability riding in a sack.

A solved problem, and a new liability riding in a sack.

The forgotten god. Asfache collapsed and dreamed of the Pool of Memories — and of something dragon-shaped and wrong feeding on it. In the same instant, the strange doll the party had carried out of Farylse stirred on its own, and the green orb Asfache had taken from an elder brain began to glow. The party brought the two together. The doll spoke: “That’s mine — it will help me… remember.” The god inside the doll woke partway, and remembered one crucial fact — its body is the Giant Robot, the five-mile clay titan. Which is not a set of directions. It cannot lead them to a thing it is: the party has to find the titan and put the god back inside it first, and only then can it lead them anywhere.

Five miles of clay, and a god asleep inside it. The doll knows what its body is now. It still has to be found.

About that orb

The green orb came out of an elder brain, and the elder brain did not hand it over.

“I’MA DROP THAT WALL!” — Sofia’s contribution, and the whole of her tactical doctrine.

Asfache fishing Rat back out of the fluid. Nobody has ever said what the fluid was.

It was now Winter of Year 2. The Dragonsmoot was waiting.
