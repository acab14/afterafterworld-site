Steven

Lizardfolk. Prophet of Essyliss, DwarvoDraco, founder of LIMA.

Steven is the prophet of Essyliss, goddess of devotion, and the DwarvoDraco — the chosen one of the lizardfolk. He founded LIMA, his order of Essylissian volunteers, immediately after assassinating the shapeshifter posing as mayor of New Dwarf City, and he holds the writ to silence the Siren Song: the party’s actual win condition, carried in a prophet’s pack.

His faith runs on ten tenets and the Everscale, the scale Essyliss gave him, which carries his boons. Under the Craving — the meat-hunger Ghroth’s bite left in him — he broke the vegetarian tenet — permanently — and surrendered the Everscale to a dying Essyliss, losing his boons with it. He spent months as a prophet with vows and no way to speak them.

Known for. Taking the New Dwarf City Dwarfway on tour through the lizardfolk tribes (Jersey Dwarfs, Dwarf Lightning, Chicago, The Lizard of the Opera). Raising the writ in the First Run’s last battle and stopping the Siren Song — it worked, and it was not enough. Defending Essyliss publicly at the Dragonsmoot, and learning in the same breath that the goddess he served is a member of the World Serpent.

The reconciliation. At LIMA an elf in white left the room the instant she saw him. He gave chase, lost her, asked around his own organization, and came away with a name: Sienna. Sienna is Essyliss. They had it out and then they mended it, quietly and in both directions, in a room with Sofia lying a few feet away.

His new tenth tenet is “I am forgiven” — the only one of the ten that requires somebody else to agree, and she agreed to it. Hers is “I will not give up.” She gave the Everscale back. Then she told him the war against Ghroth was already lost, and he told her about Innominandum, and she changed her mind.

Where he stands now. He served as third hand on the con of Tiamat, then looked in on Sofia and found two goddesses arguing over her bed. He owes the party that conversation. He is on the assault over Avernus with the rest of them, kit back, and the Shadowfell after that.
