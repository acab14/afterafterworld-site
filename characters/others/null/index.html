Null

The third dragon god. Sealed in an amulet. Released once, by this party, with results.

Null is the third of IO’s extensions, alongside Bahamut and Tiamat, and the only one of the three who is not at large. He is sealed in an amulet, and he has been for a very long time.

The worst decision anybody made

In the Fall of Year 3 of the First Run, Rat slipped into Tiamat’s tomb in Avernus and stole the amulet. He carried it back to the mortal plane, and Null came out.

What followed was ruin. Null tore loose on a revenge rampage against his own siblings, killed gods, and destabilized the divine line holding at the Edge of Existence at the exact moment that line could least afford it. Mordenkainen had quietly intended to use the amulet — to carve a refuge out of the Null Realm, or to ransom it to Bahamut. Every one of those plans ended in the same hour.

It is the single worst decision anyone in the party has ever made, and undoing it was one of the first things they did with their second chance.

This time

In the Second Run, Carxi was dispatched to retrieve the amulet before Rat’s first-run self could ever bring it to the mortal plane. It went to Mordenkainen, who began working it into an escape route.

At the Dragonsmoot, IO noticed the amulet was gone — and then began to say something about Null, and never finished it, because Thoth pulled the party out of the assembly mid-sentence. Every dragon at that gathering heard the rest of the sentence. The party did not.

Where the amulet is now

With Tiamat. The party handed the real amulet over off a counterfeit corpse in Limbo, as the price of a dragon queen’s departure.

So Null’s prison now rides with the sister who helped seal him, on the errand of the father whose intentions toward it nobody in the party has actually heard.

The party is the only faction alive that has watched an unsealed Null end a war.
