Heart Melts

Clockwork dragon. Ruler of Newport. The one who broke the wall.

Heart Melts was a clockwork dragon and the ruler of Newport, and he is the reason there is a clock on any of this.

His army was the igloids — clockwork drones by the thousand, run through his Orb of Consciousness — and they were the force that ringed Wip in the siege the party walked into on their first day in the city. His family held the commands: Pyre, Prodigy, and Perception Melts.

Then he went to Jun. He came down through the roof of the temple, into the ziggurat beneath it, and destroyed the Jun Ley Crystal — one of the stones in the wall the gods built between the world and the Far Realm. Aberrations poured through the gap. King Ramses survived it, gravely wounded. Only the Wip crystal was left, with roughly five years in it, and the doomsday clock started that afternoon.

He also took Cal, pulled into the Orb of Consciousness.

The party killed him at the Battle of the Jun Ziggurat. The Orb shattered with him — which freed the igloid army into minds of its own, and left Cal’s fate unknown. When the party jumped back five years, they landed after that fight: Heart Melts is dead in both runs, and nothing they have done since has had to account for him.

What is left of him is the family and the city. Newport is an ally now — trade restored, the siege a piece of old history — and the Melts sit inside R4’s Council of United Members, allied by necessity and old arithmetic.

Gallery

The Battle of the Jun Ziggurat. Six people, a temple floor with a hole in it, and the thing that had just come down through it. They killed him — and the Orb of Consciousness shattered with him, which is why the igloid army woke up with minds of its own, and why nobody has found Cal since.

One of his war machines. Newport built to a scale nobody else on the continent was building to, and this is what the party was expected to stand in front of.
