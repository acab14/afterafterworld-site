Characters

Who is holding the line, who they answer to, who let them try — and what is coming through the wall.

The Party

The nine who lost this war once already and came back to do it again.

Rat — Champion of Thoth. Officially dead.

R4 — Champion of Aditi. Rules Wip behind a borrowed face.

Ralf — Six minds, one metal body. La Bomba.

Smolder — Smut Shaq, the Underdark, and Catman.

Asfache — Champion of Kratos. Seer of the Pool.

Una — Young golden dragon. Dracogenesis.

Steven — Prophet of Essyliss. Holds the writ.

Bram — Bard of Yore. The one who killed a prince.

Sofia — The Ent Mother. Retired.

Dragons & the World Serpent

A raspy, broken voice called every dragon in creation to one field in the Outlands — the first Dragonsmoot in ten thousand years — and told them he means to remake the World Serpent. The party has one of them in the family.

IO (Asgorath) — Called them all. Then stood in the field alone.

Tiamat — Came for the amulet. Got it.

Bahamut — Searching the planes for a goddess who has already been found.

Null — The third dragon god. Sealed.

Meiko — Ancient gold. Went recruiting. Not back.

Oman — Una’s father. The flame she was forged in.

Kereska — Dragon goddess of magic. Knew the old rite.

Vessa — Copper chronicler. Smelled the receipt.

The Home Front

The war is not only being fought by the six. Somebody is holding the city, somebody is holding the west, and somebody built every door they travel through — and two of those somebodies have stopped answering their post.

Luna Cantine — Matriarch of the Wolves. Now intermediary Queen of Wip.

Ragar Huntsfellow — Invented the La Bomba. Freed R4.

Alamil — Built every door. Missing.

Catroniel — Rat’s mentor. Holds the west.

Ulaxium — Former President of the Arcane University. Not answering.

Emma Wip — The Queen who died. The face that didn’t.

King Ramses Jun — The Staff of Isis and seven healing stones.

Sinidil & Ligma — The Underdark, held by a favor.

Bast, Horus & Nubia — The Monks of Jun. Two of three spent for good.

The Junish Company — Baz’s people, who outlived him. Willamet holds Mopak.

Allies, Rivals & Others

Not the party, not their patrons, not the thing behind the wall — everyone else the war has put in the room. A god keeping a bar. A rakshasa who has been watching for two years. An archmage who is also the ride home. A piece of grey weather that will not leave.

Thoril — Killed Orcus. Spending himself in halves.

Caspian — Took Avernus. Currently under fire.

Mordenkainen — Built the corpse. Named the trap. The ride home.

Marvin — The Feywild’s seal. Whole, and gone.

Callahan — The Shadowfell’s seal. The clock is him.

The Cat Lord & Felina — Gave the verdict. Gave the bell.

The Witness — Watches Rat. Says nothing. Decides everything.

Carxi — Rat’s protégé. Died once, professionally.

Malachas — Arch Devil. Freed twice. Punctual.

The White Lion — Wrote the writ. Twice. Under protest.

Flamencio — The Artist who paints the door.

Baz Stricknaz — Died at Saki. Still following Bram.

Rodney — Fifteen years in an afternoon.

Dak’kon — Caught watching. Asked for help.

Nala & Raja — Traded for body parts. Run the franchise.

Ohn, in the Doll — The forgotten god. Getting smaller.

Cal & Memory-Cal — One missing. One remembered.

The Faces of Farylse — Gloom, Regret, Shatter.

Dryzzlazz — The Spider Queen. Cured twice.

Pentad-Nine — The modron that changed its mind.

Heart Melts — The clockwork dragon who broke the Jun crystal.
