La Bombara

Recipe only
Ralf’s notes · the greater bomb

Built once, in the run that was lost. Nine hundred missiles in a receptacle cut from Ley Crystal — one detonation, and nine unbroken minutes to arm it. You hold the knowledge. You do not hold a crystal.

How one goes off

Reminders, before somebody improvises. The one holding the orb must be a caster with a first-level spell slot — the orb does not care which spells you know, only that magic answers you. Arming it takes one action of pure concentration per ten missiles: no movement, no bonus action, no reaction, nothing else, or the working fails. And the release must come the instant the count is done, or the whole cast collapses — though a spoiled cast does not spend the bomb. There are no fuses and no timers. A La Bomba goes off because somebody stood still in the middle of everything, long enough to finish. Decide who that is, and where they stand, before the moment finds you.

There is a rakshasa across the room and four mirrors that disagree with him, a courier asleep in his sleeve with your worst secret in it, a githzerai waiting for an answer, and a dead man four inches tall in the wall who has just been told he is dead.
