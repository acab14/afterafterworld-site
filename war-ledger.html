Steven

Prophet of Essyliss · LIMA founder

Held the Mirrorworks together with a light no fiend could look at. Then raised an archmage’s lover from the dead with a chicken dance and the words we are lizard, for an hour, and it worked. She looked at the old man at the foot of the bed and asked who he was. The Everscale is warm. Nothing is owed to anyone.

Smolder

Underdark operator · entrepreneur

Put a devil into a floor, was pulled into a mirror and came back out through it, flew after a soul. Then did not sleep: a sea, a terrified boy, ten potions on the crown’s tab, and the heart of a god, where he raised his arms and green light came down into him and a five-mile statue learned to fly. It took something. He knows that.

Una

Golden dragon · heir of Dracogenesis

Emptied a room of devils by suggesting they go and fetch water. Said a rakshasa’s true name and he stopped with his hands on the way out. Asked the archmage to find the letter; he could not. His advice was find Tiamat. The cold glass ball is still in her pack.

Asfache

Champion of Kratos · seer of the Pool

The Armory — Ralf’s Parting Gift

Field grade

×8
steel core · 24 missiles

24d4+24 force in a 20-foot blast, thrown up to 140 feet. Two rounds of stillness, then the throw. The only grade you can realistically set off while something is trying to kill you.

Officer grade

×4
gold core · 54 missiles

54d4+54 force in a 50-foot blast, 170 feet of range. Five full rounds of unbroken concentration. Bring friends who can hold a perimeter.

Siege grade

×3
gem core · 105 missiles

105d4+105 force in a 100-foot blast, 220 feet of range. Ten rounds of doing absolutely nothing else. Not a combat action — a decision about a building.

La Bombara

Recipe only
Ralf’s notes · the greater bomb

Built once, in the run that was lost. Nine hundred missiles in a receptacle cut from Ley Crystal — one detonation, and nine unbroken minutes to arm it. You hold the knowledge. You do not hold a crystal.

How one goes off

Reminders, before somebody improvises. The one holding the orb must be a caster with a first-level spell slot — the orb does not care which spells you know, only that magic answers you. Arming it takes one action of pure concentration per ten missiles: no movement, no bonus action, no reaction, nothing else, or the working fails. And the release must come the instant the count is done, or the whole cast collapses — though a spoiled cast does not spend the bomb. There are no fuses and no timers. A La Bomba goes off because somebody stood still in the middle of everything, long enough to finish. Decide who that is, and where they stand, before the moment finds you.
